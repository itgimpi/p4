Date su rečenice:

Nobody who really appreciates Beethoven fails to keep silence while the Moonlight sonata is being played.
Guinea pigs are hopelessly ignorant of music.
No one who is hopelessly ignorant of music ever keeps silence while the Moonlight sonata is being played.
Therefore, guinea pigs never really appreciate Beethoven.

Uvedimo formule:
Beethoven(x) - x appreciates Beethoven
Silent(x) - x is silent (keeps silence) while the Moonlight sonata is being played
GuineaP ig(x) - x is a guinea pig
Ignorant(x) - x is hopelessly ignorant of music

Prevedimo date rečenice koristeći ove formule:
∀x(Beethoven(x) ⇒ Silent(x))
∀x(GuineaP ig(x) ⇒ Ignorant(x))
∀x(Ignorant(x) ⇒ ¬Silent(x))
